# Manufacturing, inspection & assembly

> **Key concept** · The manufacturing route is selected based on geometry, alloy, tolerances and production requirements.

Forging, casting, machining and surface treatments may be possible routes, but none is universal. LE/TE form, surface finish, trunnion concentricity and travel range may require process-specific checks.

Assembly may involve bushing fit, lever attachment, stop adjustment, synchronization and torque checks. Traceability, inspection methods and drawing revisions are part of configuration control.

<EngineeringNote>
Drawing practices must capture both aerodynamic geometry and pivot/interface tolerances.
</EngineeringNote>
