# Failure mechanisms & inspection

> **Key concept** · VSV failure mechanisms can affect aerodynamic performance, positioning capability and mechanical integrity.

Potential risks include airfoil cracks, erosion/FOD, trunnion wear, bushing damage, fretting, loosened levers/linkages, synchronization error and sticking. These are general mechanisms, not a statement of observed failures on a named engine.

Actual inspection and maintenance instructions must come from approved engine documentation. Visual checks, clearance assessments, torque/position verification and appropriate NDT are educational examples only.

<EngineeringNote>
Never publish specific maintenance limits without authoritative engine-specific documentation.
</EngineeringNote>
