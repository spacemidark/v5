# Design review checklist

> **Key concept** · VSV design requires coordinated aerodynamic, mechanical and controls reviews.

Aerodynamics: Which operating points require reset? How are incidence, compressor maps and surge margin validated?

Mechanics: How are load envelopes, pivot reactions, bushing life, maximum actuation torque, tolerance stack-up and thermal jamming evaluated?

System: How are commanded versus achieved angles verified? What are the failure-safe and maintainability considerations?

<EngineeringNote>
This is a starting checklist, not a substitute for formal requirements or certification evidence.
</EngineeringNote>
