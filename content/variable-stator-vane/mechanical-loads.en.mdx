# Mechanical loads & structural integrity

> **Key concept** · Structural assessment includes distributed aerodynamic pressure loads and forces/moments transferred through pivots, supports and linkages.

Aerodynamic pressure distribution induces vane bending, torsion and support reactions. Actuation torque and friction add further loads during motion; vibration and thermal deformation can change combined load cases.

Assessment includes load envelopes, stress concentrations, root/trunnion fillets, contact stresses, deflection and fatigue. The proper methods and acceptance limits depend on component design and material.

Draw the load path explicitly: airfoil → trunnion → bushing/casing and/or lever → linkage.
