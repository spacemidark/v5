# Actuation system & kinematics

> **Key concept** · A VSV is both an aerodynamic component and part of a moving mechanical system.

Depending on architecture, components may include an actuator, linkage, unison ring, actuating lever, vane trunnion, bearing/bushing and mechanical stops. A unison ring is a common but not universal way to synchronize multiple vanes.

Kinematic checks include lever ratios, travel direction, synchronization error, backlash, jamming, thermal offsets, and tolerance stack-up. Required torque is evaluated against actuator capability with relevant margins.

<EngineeringNote>
An adequate vane airfoil is not enough; the mechanism must remain functional over the service envelope.
</EngineeringNote>
