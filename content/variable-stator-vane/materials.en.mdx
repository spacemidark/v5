# Materials, coatings & selection

> **Key concept** · There is no universally correct VSV material; selection depends on location, temperature, environment and loading history.

Depending on the design, candidate vane-body families may include titanium alloys, steels or nickel-base alloys at higher temperatures. These are possibilities, not claims about a particular engine without documentation.

For bushings and interfaces, assess wear, friction, mating materials, lubrication conditions and environment independently. Coatings, surface hardening and surface finish follow interface-specific requirements.

<EngineeringNote>
Material selection balances more than temperature capability: fatigue, corrosion, manufacturability, maintenance and cost.
</EngineeringNote>
