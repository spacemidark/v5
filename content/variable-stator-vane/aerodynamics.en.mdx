# Aerodynamics & velocity triangles

> **Key concept** · A VSV changes the absolute velocity direction presented to the downstream rotor and thus affects its relative inlet velocity triangle.

In simplified vector form, W = V − U, where W is rotor-relative velocity, V is absolute velocity, and U is rotor blade speed. Changing vane exit flow direction changes the relative inlet triangle for a given U.

Relevant design quantities include incidence, deviation, diffusion, separation, profile loss, endwall/secondary-flow loss and blade-row interaction. Benefits and permissible ranges are engine specific and require verification.

<EngineeringNote>
Geometric stagger angle is not identical to exit flow angle; deviation and losses matter.
</EngineeringNote>
