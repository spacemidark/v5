# Bushings, friction & wear

> **Key concept** · Long-term motion quality often depends on small contact components and tribological interfaces.

Trunnion/bushing micromotion, contact pressure, material pairing, contamination and lubrication assumptions need assessment. Dry-running and coated interface concepts may exist depending on architecture; they should not be assumed without evidence.

Wear can increase clearance and angle hysteresis or raise friction torque. These are functional control and maintainability concerns, not only strength concerns.

<EngineeringNote>
Consider small-amplitude cyclic motion and dwell conditions, not only maximum actuation force.
</EngineeringNote>
