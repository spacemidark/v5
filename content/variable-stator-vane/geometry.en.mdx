# Airfoil & mechanical geometry

> **Key concept** · Airfoil, pivot/trunnion region and adjacent platforms are constrained by aerodynamic and mechanical requirements.

Primary geometric parameters include chord, span, camber, inlet/outlet metal angles, stagger, leading-edge radius, thickness distribution, and solidity. Pivot-axis location also affects clearance and loading as the vane turns.

Mechanical details include trunnion diameters, bushing interfaces, mechanical stops, and lever attachment features. Interference checks must cover the full commanded motion range.

<EngineeringNote>
Pivot location relative to the aerodynamic loading distribution affects transmitted moment.
</EngineeringNote>
