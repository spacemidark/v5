# Vibration, resonance & fatigue

> **Key concept** · Vibration assessment evaluates the vane and support dynamics together with their excitation environment.

Blade-passing-order excitation and other unsteady pressures interact with support stiffness, damping and interfaces. A change in vane setting can change aerodynamic forcing and contact conditions, so relevant settings should be included.

Depending on design, HCF/LCF, fretting fatigue and combined load histories should be assessed using validated material data and appropriate criteria.

<EngineeringNote>
A VSV is a stator component, not a spinning rotor blade; its dominant loading mechanisms can be different.
</EngineeringNote>
