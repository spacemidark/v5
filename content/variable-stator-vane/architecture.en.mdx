# Place in compressor architecture

> **Key concept** · Variable stator rows are selected where variable geometry benefits operability; no universal row count applies.

The flowpath contains casing, rotor rows, stator rows, platforms and seal interfaces. Variable vanes may be supported at their pivots by hardware integrated with the compressor casing.

One or several vane rows can be coordinated by a control mechanism. Aerodynamic requirements and actuation kinematics therefore need to be developed together.

<EngineeringNote>
Avoid assigning fixed row numbers without identifying the engine architecture.
</EngineeringNote>
