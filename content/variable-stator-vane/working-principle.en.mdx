# Operating principle & schedule

> **Key concept** · VSV setting angles are scheduled to adapt stage inlet conditions at different speeds and operating points.

The preferred incidence angles of different stages change with operating point. Variable settings can assist in managing incidence, losses and stall/surge margin over portions of the operating range.

The actual schedule is established using analysis, testing, and engine-control requirements. Commanded vane angle and achieved vane angle can differ due to backlash, elastic deformation, or friction.

<EngineeringNote>
An “open” setting is not universally better; the optimum depends on operating point.
</EngineeringNote>
