# Thermal effects, clearances & tolerances

> **Key concept** · Operating temperature and differential expansion influence gaps and friction in movable VSV interfaces.

Casing, vane, bushing, lever and ring may have different temperatures and expansion rates. A suitable cold clearance can shrink at operating temperature, potentially increasing friction or jamming risk; excessive clearance can degrade setting accuracy.

Checks should span tolerance extremes, transient thermal gradients, constrained expansion and the applicable operating envelope, not only nominal room-temperature dimensions.
