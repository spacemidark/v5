# Turbofan Engine

In a turbofan, inlet air meets the fan. Part flows through the bypass duct while the core stream enters the compressors, gains heat in the combustor, and expands through turbines before leaving through the exhaust. Turbine shaft work must match the compressor and fan work demanded by their spools.

> **Content status:** This encyclopedia uses a generic two-spool turbofan as an educational reference. Actual engines may differ in spool count, reduction gearing, bleed architecture, VSV installation and thrust-reverser layout.
