# Manufacturing & tolerances

> **Key concept** · The manufacturing route must deliver repeatable airfoil shape, adequate surface integrity and robust attachment geometry.

Depending on material and assembly architecture, candidate processes include forging plus machining, casting plus finish machining, machining from billet, and fabrication/assembly. The correct route is part-specific: not every process suits every vane or temperature class.

Control important-to-performance geometry such as profile, metal angles, leading/trailing edge thickness, root fillets, platforms, datum structure and surface texture. Machining-induced residual stress or defects can alter fatigue behaviour.

Link measurement strategy to drawing datums and functional requirements. CMM, optical scanning, surface inspection and qualified NDT are possible tools, selected by risk and material.

<EngineeringNote>
A dimension is useful only when its inspection method, datum scheme and acceptance criteria are defined.
</EngineeringNote>
