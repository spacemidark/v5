# Aerodynamic design

> **Key concept** · Turning effectiveness must be balanced against profile, endwall, secondary-flow and mixing losses.

The design process starts with the target inlet/outlet flow angles, mass flow, corrected speed, radial distribution and expected off-design operation. Incidence mismatch can promote suction-side separation and increased loss. High diffusion is attractive for compact compressors but excessive diffusion can worsen loss and stall susceptibility.

Airfoil profile, camber, chord, pitch, thickness, stagger and spanwise stacking influence turning and loss. Annulus-wall boundary layers produce three-dimensional secondary flows, so a two-dimensional mid-span section is not a complete design.

Solidity σ = c/s uses chord c and circumferential pitch s. Aspect ratio AR = h/c expresses span relative to chord. Both influence performance but neither has a universal “best” value.

<EngineeringNote>
A good midspan cascade result cannot by itself close a full-annulus loss or operability assessment.
</EngineeringNote>
