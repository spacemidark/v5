# Working principle

> **Key concept** · A stator redirects the absolute flow leaving a rotor toward the incidence range required by the next rotor.

A rotor transfers energy to the air and often leaves an appreciable circumferential velocity component. The stator modifies swirl and distributes velocity across the annulus, helping the downstream rotor encounter a useful inlet flow angle.

For a steady, nearly adiabatic fixed stator without external shaft work, stagnation enthalpy h₀ is approximately conserved. Viscous losses cause stagnation pressure p₀ to decrease; static pressure p may nevertheless increase as the flow diffuses. These are different thermodynamic quantities.

<EngineeringNote>
“Stator increases pressure” is incomplete: specify static pressure, total pressure, and the operating point.
</EngineeringNote>
