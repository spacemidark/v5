# Failure mechanisms

> **Key concept** · Failure analysis should connect the observed damage to a plausible physical mechanism and its root cause.

Potential mechanisms include high-cycle fatigue from flow excitation, low-cycle thermal/mechanical fatigue where cyclic stresses warrant it, foreign-object impact, leading-edge erosion, corrosion, fretting at retained interfaces and rub-related damage. Relative importance depends strongly on configuration.

Inspection findings such as cracking, coating loss or wear are symptoms—not automatic root causes. Investigate loading history, modal response, material condition, assembly deviations and operating environment before selecting corrective action.

<EngineeringNote>
Identify mechanism → evidence → severity → corrective action → verification, not just “replace the vane.”
</EngineeringNote>
