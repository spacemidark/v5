# Verification & inspection

> **Key concept** · A defensible technical chapter separates design intent, analysis evidence, measurement and acceptance criteria.

Aerodynamic verification may require cascade/ring-rig/engine testing and validated CFD, depending on the claim. Structural verification can include FEA, modal testing, fatigue evidence and instrumentation. Manufacturing verification checks geometry, NDT results, process qualification and traceability.

Record the exact boundary conditions, revision, component definition, data provenance and uncertainties. “Pass/fail” requires a requirement and acceptance threshold, neither of which should be invented for this educational prototype.

<EngineeringNote>
An engineering note is not an approved requirement, drawing, stress report or certification statement.
</EngineeringNote>
