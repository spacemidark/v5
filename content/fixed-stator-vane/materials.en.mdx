# Materials & selection

> **Key concept** · Material choice depends on temperature, fatigue, damage tolerance, stiffness, corrosion, weight, cost and inspectability.

Steels and nickel-based alloys are established compressor stator material families; titanium alloys may be suitable for particular cooler-stage applications. Material use is not universal: part, location, temperature limit, environment, interface pairing and engine-specific certification matter.

Fatigue strength after foreign-object damage and notch sensitivity can be as important as nominal yield strength. A coating or surface treatment may be chosen for erosion, oxidation or friction control, but every coating introduces process and inspection requirements.

Do not assign an exact alloy designation or allowable without a documented engine, stage, material specification and temperature-dependent property source.

<EngineeringNote>
Property values must match temperature, product form, heat treatment and environmental exposure; a generic online material card is insufficient for compliance.
</EngineeringNote>
