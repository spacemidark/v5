# References & evidence

> **Key concept** · Follow the source trail and keep claims traceable. The original diagrams in this prototype are schematic and not real-engine drawings.

Primary sources on compressor aerodynamics include NASA SP-36 and NASA TP-1523. NASA stator studies address controlled-diffusion designs and endwall flow. For materials, the Cambridge material overview provides context but is not an allowable database.

Before publishing engine-specific alloy, dimensions, life, clearances or acceptance criteria, attach a specific design record or openly licensed primary reference. Preserve author, version, year, access rights and component applicability.
