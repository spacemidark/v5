# Thermal behaviour & clearances

> **Key concept** · Temperature gradients and differential expansion can change stress, fit and clearances throughout the operating envelope.

An unconstrained uniform material would expand by ΔL = αLΔT for a linear approximation with a suitable average thermal expansion coefficient. Real hardware has temperature gradients, different material properties and mechanical constraints, so this equation is only an introductory estimate.

Evaluate cold build, steady hot running, acceleration/deceleration and abnormal thermal transients separately. Relative thermal movement between stator assemblies, shrouds, casing and rotor can alter rub risk, seal effectiveness or contact loading.
