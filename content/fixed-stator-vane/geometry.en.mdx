# Geometry & nomenclature

> **Key concept** · Airfoil geometry converts flow requirements into a manufacturable, inspectable component.

The leading edge (LE) first encounters the flow, while the trailing edge (TE) controls the exit wake and contributes to deviation. Pressure and suction surfaces determine the pressure distribution. Chord, camber line, metal inlet/outlet angles and maximum thickness must be defined in a consistent coordinate system.

A 3D stator may use lean, sweep, twist, variable thickness and different hub/shroud end treatments. Interface geometry and tolerances can dominate installation risk even if the airfoil satisfies CFD targets.

A real engineering drawing would also define datums, profile tolerances, surface finish, fillet radii, platform dimensions, inspection zones, and traceability. The prototype diagrams are explanatory, not production drawings.
